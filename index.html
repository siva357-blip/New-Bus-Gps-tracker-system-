Where each OOP concept is used

  • Encapsulation: Vehicle keeps #id and #number private. Route keeps #stops private.
  • Inheritance: Bus extends Vehicle.
  • Polymorphism: Bus overrides describe().
  • Abstraction: BusRepository hides all SQL from the rest of the app.
  • Composition: a Route has many Stop objects, and a Bus has a Route and a Driver.

Demo data: the database runs inside your browser and bus positions are simulated.
